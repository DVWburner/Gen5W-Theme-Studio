Gen5W Theme Studio — User Guide & Car Installation Instructions

1. Creating & Switching Themes

  • Factory Stock OEM is the built-in baseline showing your car's unmodified dark theme.
  • Click + New Blank (Stock) to start a brand-new theme from Factory Stock, or + Save As New... to clone your current theme under a new name.
  • Use the Theme dropdown at the top-left to switch between your saved themes at any time. Each theme keeps its own separate uploaded images and settings inside studio/themes/<theme-name>/.

2. Customizing Wallpapers, Tiles, Icons & Text

  • 1. Wallpapers Tab: Upload custom 1920×720 wallpapers for the All-Apps screen and the Home Split-Screen. On the Home Split-Screen, a transparent window is automatically cut out on the right side so your live Navigation Map shows through.
  • 2. Icons, Text & Tiles Tab: Click any icon on the right-hand preview canvas (or in the left-hand grid) to select it:
    • Custom Text Label: Rename any icon (use | for a 2-line wrap), change its font size/color/position, or check Hide Text Label on This Tile for an icon-only look.
    • Independent Tile Background: Give any single icon its own uploaded tile background image, custom card fill/border, or transparent tile, or let it use the Global Tile Card style.
    • Custom Icon Graphic: Upload a replacement PNG/JPG/WebP icon or drag-and-drop an image file directly onto the 1920×720 canvas.
  • 3. Home EV Widget Tab: Keep the Factory Stock EV battery graphic, upload your own custom battery graphic, or use a vector gauge.

3. Building & Putting the Theme on a USB Drive

  1. Click the green Build & Download USB ZIP button at the top-right.
  2. Wait ~10–15 seconds while the Studio renders all 330 WebP assets and builds your signed package. Your browser will download Gen5W-Theme-<name>-USB.zip (also saved in the dist/ folder).
  3. Format a USB flash drive as FAT32 or exFAT and extract the contents of that ZIP file directly to the root of the USB drive so the USB drive contains: 
    USB_DRIVE:\
 ├── NEMESIS.data
 └── packages\
      └── gen5w.nemesis.themer.zip

4. Installing & Using on the Car (100% Safe RAM Overlay)

  1. Requires NEMESIS installed on your Gen5W headunit. Plug the USB drive into the car's data USB port and install/update the package from the NEMESIS package installer.
  2. Once installed, you can unplug and remove the USB drive — the theme is stored inside the app on the headunit and does not need the USB drive during everyday driving.
  3. Open Gen5W Themer (palette & paintbrush icon) from the NEMESIS Launcher:
    • Tap [1. APPLY CUSTOM THEME (45s Safe Test Mode)]. Your theme mounts in volatile RAM (/mnt/asec) and restarts the Home Launcher with a 45-second safety countdown.
    • After 15 seconds of previewing your Home screen, Gen5W Themer pops back up automatically so you can tap [2. CONFIRM & KEEP THEME] to cancel the rollback timer.
    • Fail-Safe Guarantee: If anything ever goes wrong or you don't confirm within 45 seconds, it automatically reverts to 100% Factory Stock. Furthermore, rebooting the headunit (or pressing the dashboard pinhole reset) ALWAYS boots 100% unmodified Factory Stock.
