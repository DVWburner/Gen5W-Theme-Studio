## ✨ What Is Gen5W Theme Studio?

**Gen5W Theme Studio** is a two-part theming toolkit for NEMESIS-equipped Gen5W `1920×720` widescreen headunits:

1. **PC Theme Studio (Local Browser App for Windows 10/11)**:
   - A visual `1920×720` live preview canvas for designing custom **Home Split-Screen** and **All-Apps Menu** themes.
   - **Home Split-Screen Customization**:
     - Custom `1920×720` wallpaper with automatic right-side transparent cutout and selectable **Map Viewport Edge Blend** (`Soft Alpha Feather`, `Left-Edge Only Alpha Fade`, `OEM Stock Gradient Mask`, or `Hard Box Cutout`) so the live Navigation Map shows through cleanly.
     - Drag-and-drop and fine-tune sliders for the **Date & Clock** (X/Y offset + font scale), **Media / Radio Readout** (X/Y offset + font scale), and **3D EV Car Status Widget** (including Shuttlecraft Galileo `NCC-1701/7` and Wireframe EV Car).
     - Custom **Home Screen Font Family** selector (`Orbitron`, `Rajdhani`, `Chakra Petch`, `Share Tech Mono`, `Audiowide`, `Michroma`, `Exo 2`, `Oxanium`, or custom `.ttf`/`.otf` upload).
   - **All-Apps Menu Customization**:
     - Replace any of the 17 app icon graphics (PNG/JPG/WebP), customize or hide icon text labels (with custom font sizes and colors), and style individual tile card backgrounds.
   - **Sub-Menu Theming (`SystemUI`, `HKMC_Climate`, `HKMC_TAppEV3`)**:
     - **4. Top Bar**: Global status bar background & Back/Home/Menu navigation buttons.
     - **5. EV Screen**: Full-screen EV Energy Information background, bottom cards, range radar toggle, and vehicle graphic (Stock OEM, Shuttlecraft Galileo, Wireframe EV Car, 3D Battery Slab, or Custom).
     - **6. Climate & Floating Seat Warmer**: Full-screen Climate background, Floating Warmer/Ventilation popup (`640×462`), warmer/ventilation icons & 3-stage LED bars, and 3D seat graphics (including the **Star Trek Captain's Chair**).
   - **Multi-Theme APK Bundling**: Bundle multiple saved themes into a single car installer `.zip` and switch between them directly on your car's screen.
   - **Community Theme Sharing (`.gen5wtheme`)**: One-click **`📤 Export`** and **`📥 Import`** buttons package all theme settings, version metadata, and custom image assets into a single shareable `.gen5wtheme` file.

2. **On-Car Gen5W Themer App, Optional Auto-Boot & 5-Layer Failsafe Architecture**:
   - Installed via USB through the NEMESIS package manager (appears in the NEMESIS Launcher with a palette & paintbrush icon).
   - **RAM-Only (`/mnt/asec`) Overlay Architecture**: Your car's `/system` partition is **never modified on disk**. When you apply a theme, the helper script copies the stock system APKs (`Launcher_WP3.apk`, `SystemUI.apk`, `HKMC_Climate.apk`, `HKMC_TAppEV3.apk`) into volatile RAM (`/mnt/asec`), injects your theme's assets into the RAM copies, and bind-mounts them over `/system/...`.
   - **Two Ways to Apply on the Car Display**:
     - **Option 1 — 45s Safe Test Mode (`[1. TEST]`)**: Applies the theme with a 45-second deadman rollback timer. If anything goes wrong and you cannot tap confirm, the background daemon automatically unmounts the RAM overlay after 45 seconds and restores your stock launcher.
     - **Option 2 — Direct Apply & Keep (`[2. APPLY & KEEP]`)**: If you already trust a theme, you can tap **`2. APPLY & KEEP`** directly from Stock state to apply and lock in the theme immediately without waiting for the 45-second test countdown.
   - **Optional Auto-Launch on Boot (`[4. AUTO-BOOT: OFF -> ON ✓]`) & Hardware Failsafes**:
     - By default, rebooting the headunit wipes volatile RAM and boots 100% Factory Stock.
     - Once a theme is confirmed, you can optionally tap **`[4. AUTO-BOOT]`** to automatically re-mount your confirmed theme 6 seconds after every headunit boot, protected by two independent hardware failsafes:
       1. **📌 Pinhole Reset Escape Hatch (6s Boot Grace Window + 2-Strike Watchdog)**: Pressing the physical dashboard pinhole reset button during startup or within 60 seconds after the theme loads (or if the Launcher crashes twice) trips the `boot_strikes >= 2` breaker on the next boot, automatically disabling Auto-Boot and booting **100% Factory Stock**.
       2. **🔌 USB Kill-Switch (`REVERT_THEME.txt`)**: Plugging in any FAT32/exFAT USB stick containing an empty file named **`REVERT_THEME.txt`** (or `SAFE_MODE.txt` / `DISABLE_THEME.txt`) at its root immediately unmounts all theme overlays, disables Auto-Boot, and restores **100% Factory Stock** within 1–2 seconds without touching the screen.

## 🚀 Option A: Just Want to Try the LCARS Theme? (No PC Studio Required)

If you just want to try the pre-made **LCARS** theme on your NEMESIS-equipped car without running the PC Theme Studio program:

1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** page and download **`Gen5W-Theme-LCARS1-USB.zip`**.
2. Extract the contents of **`Gen5W-Theme-LCARS1-USB.zip`** directly to the root of a **FAT32** or **exFAT** USB flash drive so the folder layout looks like this:
   ```text
   USB_DRIVE:\
    ├── NEMESIS.data
    └── packages\
         └── gen5w.nemesis.themer.zip
   ```
3. Plug the USB flash drive into your car's data USB port and allow **[NEMESIS](https://github.com/xwtk/hmg_nemesis)** to install the `gen5w.nemesis.themer` package.
4. Once installed, **you can unplug the USB drive** — the theme assets are stored inside the installed app on the headunit.
5. Open the **NEMESIS App Launcher** on your headunit and tap **Gen5W Themer** (palette & paintbrush icon):
   - Select your theme at the top (if multiple themes are bundled).
   - Tap **`1. TEST THEME (45s Safe Test Mode)`** to preview it safely with an automatic 45-second revert timer, followed by **`2. APPLY & KEEP`** to lock it in — **OR** tap **`2. APPLY & KEEP`** directly to skip the test timer and apply immediately.
   - Tap **`3. RESTORE TO STOCK`** at any time to disarm Auto-Boot and reboot the display back into Factory Stock, or **`✕ EXIT TO HOME`** when finished.

## 🎨 Option B: Using the PC Theme Studio (Create, Import, or Edit Themes)

### 1. Download & Launch
1. From the **[Releases](../../releases)** page, download **`Gen5W-Theme-Studio-Redistributable.zip`** (or clone this repository).
   - *Note: `Gen5W-Theme-Studio-Redistributable.zip` is completely self-contained for Windows 10/11 (x64) and includes a portable OpenJDK 17 runtime and Android `aapt` build tools — no Java or Android SDK installation is required.*
2. Extract the `.zip` to any folder on your PC and double-click **`Start-ThemeStudio.bat`**.
3. Your browser will automatically open **`http://localhost:8470/`**.

### 2. Importing a Shared `.gen5wtheme` File (e.g., `LCARS1.gen5wtheme`)
1. Download **`LCARS1.gen5wtheme`** (available on the **[Releases](../../releases)** page) or any `.gen5wtheme` file shared by another user.
2. In the top bar of the Theme Studio browser window, click **`📥 Import`** and select the `.gen5wtheme` file.
3. All backgrounds, custom icons, coordinates, and settings will load immediately into your local `studio/themes/` folder.

### 3. Designing & Exporting Your Own Theme
- **Auto-Save to Disk**: Every change you make in the browser automatically saves to `studio/themes/<theme-id>/theme-config.json` and `studio/themes/<theme-id>/assets/` inside the program folder (so clearing your browser cache or switching browsers will never erase your themes).
- **Sharing Online (`📤 Export`)**: Click **`📤 Export`** in the top bar to download a single **`<ThemeName>.gen5wtheme`** pack containing all your assets and version metadata to share with others.
- **Building the Car Installer (`📦 Build & Download USB ZIP`)**:
  1. Click **`📦 Build & Download USB ZIP`** at the top-right of the Theme Studio.
  2. Choose which of your saved themes to bundle into the car app (you can select one or multiple themes!) and choose which theme is selected by default.
  3. Click **`🚀 Build USB ZIP`**. Your browser will download **`Gen5W-Theme-<name>-USB.zip`** (and save a copy in the `dist/` folder).
  4. Extract the contents of that `-USB.zip` onto a FAT32/exFAT USB drive and install via NEMESIS just like in **Option A** above.
