SAFETY ARCHITECTURE (RAM-ONLY OVERLAY)
--------------------------------------
- Nothing on your car's /system partition is ever permanently modified on disk.
- Themes are built and mounted strictly in volatile RAM (/mnt/asec) ONLY when
  you apply a theme inside the on-car Gen5W Themer app.
- You can test a theme with the 45-Second Deadman Timer (auto-reverts to stock
  if not confirmed within 45 seconds), or tap Button 2 ("APPLY & KEEP") directly
  to skip the 45-second timer if you already trust the theme.
- Rebooting the headunit or pressing the dashboard pinhole reset button ALWAYS
  wipes volatile RAM and boots 100% unmodified Factory Stock.


================================================================================
PART 1: DESIGNING OR IMPORTING A THEME ON YOUR WINDOWS PC
================================================================================

1. No extra software (no Java or Android SDK) is required on Windows 10 or 11.
2. Double-click `Start-ThemeStudio.bat` in this folder.
   - A local web server will start and automatically open `http://localhost:8470/`
     in your browser. (Keep the PowerShell window open while using the Studio.)
3. Importing / Exporting Community Themes (`.gen5wtheme`):
   - Click `Import` in the top bar to load a downloaded `.gen5wtheme` pack.
   - Click `Export` in the top bar to package your active theme (including all
     custom images and version metadata) into a single `.gen5wtheme` file to
     share online.
4. Customizing Your Theme:
   - All changes auto-save directly to `studio/themes/<theme-id>/` on your disk.
   - Tab 1 (Wallpapers & Map Fade): Upload 1920x720 backgrounds and choose the
     Map Viewport Edge Blend (Soft Alpha Feather, Left-Edge Alpha Fade, OEM
     Gradient Mask, or Hard Cutout).
   - Tab 2 (Icons, Text & Tiles): Click any app tile on the canvas to customize
     its icon graphic, label text, font size/color, or tile background.
   - Tab 3 (Home EV & Split-Screen Widgets): Drag the Clock, Media readout, and
     3D EV Car on the canvas, or use the fine-tune sliders to move the OEM
     "Range / mi" box and "Battery / %" readout.


================================================================================
PART 2: BUILDING & PREPARING YOUR USB DRIVE
================================================================================

1. Click `[ Build & Download USB ZIP ]` at the top-right of the Theme Studio.
2. Select which saved theme(s) you want to bundle into the car app (you can
   bundle multiple themes and switch between them on the car screen!).
3. Your browser will download `Gen5W-Theme-<name>-USB.zip` (also saved in `dist\`).
4. Extract (Unzip) the contents of `Gen5W-Theme-<name>-USB.zip` directly to the
   root of a FAT32 or exFAT USB flash drive so the drive structure looks like:

     USB_DRIVE:\
      ├── NEMESIS.data
      └── packages\
           └── gen5w.nemesis.themer.zip


================================================================================
PART 3: INSTALLING & ACTIVATING ON YOUR CAR
================================================================================

PREREQUISITE: Your Gen5W headunit must already have NEMESIS installed:
  https://github.com/xwtk/hmg_nemesis

1. Plug your prepared USB flash drive into the car's data USB port and allow
   NEMESIS to install the `gen5w.nemesis.themer` package.
2. Once installed, you can unplug the USB flash drive! The theme(s) are stored
   inside the app on the headunit.
3. Open `Gen5W Themer` (palette & paintbrush icon) from the NEMESIS Launcher:
   - Select a theme at the top (if you bundled multiple themes).
   - Tap `[ 1. TEST THEME (45s Safe Test Mode) ]` to test with a 45-second
     automatic rollback timer, OR tap `[ 2. APPLY & KEEP ]` directly to skip
     the timer and lock in the theme immediately.
   - Tap `[ 3. REVERT TO STOCK NOW ]` at any time to unmount the overlay and
     return to Factory Stock, or tap `[ X EXIT TO HOME ]` when done.
