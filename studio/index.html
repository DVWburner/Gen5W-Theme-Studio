Gen5W Theme Studio — User Guide, Auto-Boot & Failsafe Documentation

1. Creating & Switching Themes

  • Factory Stock OEM is the built-in baseline showing your car's unmodified dark theme.
  • Click + New Blank to start a brand-new theme from Factory Stock, or + Save As New... to clone your current theme under a new name.
  • Use the Theme dropdown at the top-left to switch between your saved themes at any time. Each theme keeps its own separate uploaded images and settings inside studio/themes/<theme-name>/.

2. Customizing Wallpapers, Tiles, Sub-Menus & Fonts

  • 1. Wallpapers Tab: Upload custom 1920×720 wallpapers for All-Apps and Home Split-Screen (with automatic right-side Map cutout), shift the Split-Screen Clock/Date and Media displays, and choose a custom Home Screen Font Family or upload your own .ttf/.otf font.
  • 2. Icons, Text & Tiles Tab: Click any icon on the canvas to customize its label, font size/color, per-tile background, or upload/drag-and-drop a replacement icon graphic.
  • 3. Home EV & 5. EV Screen Tabs: Choose between Factory Stock, Wireframe EV Car, Star Trek Shuttlecraft Galileo (NCC-1701/7), 3D Battery Slab, Vector Gauge, or a custom uploaded vehicle graphic.
  • 4. Top Bar & 6. Climate/Seats Tabs: Customize the global top status bar (`SystemUI`), Climate screen, Floating Seat Warmer/Ventilation popup (`HKMC_Climate`), warmer/ventilation icons & 3-stage LED bars, and 3D seat graphic (including the Star Trek Captain's Chair).

3. Building & Putting the Theme on a USB Drive

  1. Click the green Build & Download USB ZIP... button at the top-right and select which themes to bundle into the car APK.
  2. Wait ~10–15 seconds while the Studio renders all WebP assets and builds your signed package. Your browser will download Gen5W-Theme-<name>-USB.zip (also saved in the dist/ folder).
  3. Format a USB flash drive as FAT32 or exFAT and extract the contents of that ZIP file directly to the root of the USB drive so the USB drive contains: 
    USB_DRIVE:\
 ├── NEMESIS.data
 └── packages\
      └── gen5w.nemesis.themer.zip

4. Applying Themes & Enabling Auto-Launch on Boot

  1. Requires NEMESIS installed on your Gen5W headunit. Plug the USB drive into the car's data USB port and install/update the package from the NEMESIS package installer. Once installed, you can unplug the USB drive.
  2. Open Gen5W Themer from the NEMESIS Launcher:
    • Step 1 — Safe Test Mode (45s Rollback Timer): Tap [1. TEST '<THEME>'] to mount your theme in RAM (/mnt/asec) with a 45-second deadman countdown. If you don't confirm within 45 seconds, it automatically reverts to 100% Factory Stock.
    • Step 2 — Confirm & Keep: Tap [2. APPLY & KEEP] to cancel the 45-second timer (or apply a known-good theme directly).
    • Step 3 — Restore to Stock: Tap [3. RESTORE TO STOCK] at any time to immediately disarm Auto-Boot and reboot the display back into 100% Factory Stock (or follow the pin-hole reset prompt if software reboot is blocked).
    • Step 4 — Auto-Boot on Reboot (Optional): Once a theme is active and confirmed, tap [4. AUTO-BOOT: OFF → ON ✓] to cache your confirmed theme so it launches automatically 6 seconds after every headunit boot! (Note: the /system flash partition is still never modified on disk—Auto-Boot still uses safe RAM bind-mounts.)

5. Emergency Failsafes (Pinhole Reset & USB Kill-Switch)

Even when [4. AUTO-BOOT: ON ✓] is enabled, two independent hardware failsafes ensure you can always return to 100% Factory Stock without touching the screen:
  • 📌 Pinhole Reset Escape Hatch (6s Boot Grace Window + 2-Strike Watchdog):
    On every boot with Auto-Boot enabled, the background service records a boot-attempt strike on disk before doing anything, waits a 6-second grace window after Android finishes booting, mounts the theme, and then monitors the Home Launcher for 60 continuous seconds before resetting the strike counter to 0.
    → How to trigger: Press the physical dashboard pinhole reset button during startup (or within 60 seconds after the theme loads). If a boot is interrupted or the Launcher crashes twice before passing the 60-second stability check (boot_strikes ≥ 2), the next boot automatically trips the failsafe breaker, disables Auto-Boot, and boots 100% Factory Stock.
  • 🔌 USB "Panic / Safe-Mode" Kill-Switch (Zero-Touch Hardware Recovery):
    Create an empty file named REVERT_THEME.txt (or SAFE_MODE.txt / DISABLE_THEME.txt) at the root of any FAT32/exFAT USB flash drive:
    USB_DRIVE:\REVERT_THEME.txt
    → How to trigger: Plug that USB stick into the car's USB port (either before turning the car on, or at any time while the headunit is running). Within 1–2 seconds, the root helper service detects the file, immediately unmounts all theme overlays, disables Auto-Boot, and restores 100% Factory Stock without needing any touchscreen input.
